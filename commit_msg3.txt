Add dealer_gamma module: market-maker hedging spiral (V9 P1)

Mechanism model of the short-gamma feedback loop: when dealers are net
short gamma, a selloff forces mechanical delta-hedge selling that deepens
the fall. Net gamma approximated from VIX/SKEW state (free data), params
configurable. Self-test asserts short-gamma amplifies, long-gamma damps,
intervention reduces amplification, determinism.
