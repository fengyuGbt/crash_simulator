V9-P3: discrete margin & threshold bunching

Mechanism response to the Part-4 reader comment: forced selling is not
a continuous ODE. Brokers mark at discrete intervals, and the next
threshold sits on a round number that many accounts share - so a
10% print can skip 12 and land at 18 before cash can be added.

- Discrete marking every N steps (vs system_dynamics' smooth flow)
- Integer-level threshold buckets (-10/-15/-20/-25), batch liquidation
- Same-mark cascades: a -15% print skips three levels in one mark
  round and lands near -24% before anyone can react
- Liquidation-window metric (steps + cumulative flow) - the part a
  VaR that treats the Fed as a residual never prices
- Optional backstop (2020-style: cut the flow, not the news):
  absorbs forced-selling flow, shallows the drop, speeds recovery
- Self-test: 6 invariants green (small-shock safety, steps-not-slope,
  loss concentration, window measurable, determinism, intervention)
